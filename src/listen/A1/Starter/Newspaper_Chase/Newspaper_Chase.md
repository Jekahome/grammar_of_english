  <div class="chapter-title">The Painting</div>
  <div class="chapter-divider"><span> ~ ~ ~ </span></div>
  <div class="book-body">
<p class="first-para">The time is one o'clock in the morning. The place is the Ritesville town art gallery.</p>
<p>A window opens and a man comes in. His name is Harry Black, and he is a thief.</p>
<p>It is dark in the art gallery, but Harry has a light. He looks across the room at a painting.</p>
<p>"There it is!" he says.</p>
<p>Harry moves quickly across the room. He stands and looks at the painting.</p>
<p>"A million dollars for this?" he thinks. "I don't understand it."</p>
<p>But he takes a knife from his coat.</p>
<p>Then he takes the painting very, very slowly from its frame.</p>
<p>Harry goes back across the room to the window, but he walks into a table. There is a beautiful blue glass vase on the table. It falls on the floor and breaks into a hundred pieces.</p>
<p>Harry smiles. "Is that a million-dollar vase?" he thinks. "It isn't now!"</p>
<p>He runs across the pieces of glass to the window.</p>
<p>Harry has a room in Mrs. Allen's rooming house. He goes quietly up to his room and closes the door.</p>
<p>Mrs. Allen and her daughter, Janey, are sleeping. They don't hear him.</p>
<p>In his room, Harry takes the painting from his bag. He puts it in a newspaper, then he puts the newspaper under his bed.</p>

<summary>Chapter Two</summary>
<div class="book-page">
  <div class="chapter-label">Chapter Two</div>
  <div class="chapter-title">The Newspaper</div>
  <div class="chapter-divider"><span> ~ ~ ~ </span></div>
  <div class="book-body">
<p class="first-para">In the morning, Janey Allen is in the kitchen. She is putting old bottles into a box.</p>
<p>"Recycling is important," Janey thinks.</p>
<p>On the TV, a reporter is at the Ritesville art gallery. He is talking about the painting.</p>
<p>"It's a million-dollar painting," he is saying. "Here's a photo of it."</p>
<p>Now the reporter is talking about the blue glass vase. "It's in a hundred pieces now," he says.</p>
<p>Janey looks at the photo of the vase, then she asks her mother, "Do you have any old bottles?"</p>
<p>"No," Mrs. Allen says. "That's all, Janey. But the newspaper recycling truck is coming today."</p>
<p>"Of course!" Janey says. "It's Friday!"</p>
<p>Harry isn't in his room. He is talking on his telephone to a man in Seattle. The man wants the painting, but Harry isn't happy.</p>
<p>"Five thousand dollars?" Harry says. "No! It's a million-dollar painting! ... What? ... No, I want fifty thousand, not five! ... What? ... The painting? Yes, I have it, and it's OK."</p>
<p>Janey is looking for old newspapers. Early on Friday mornings, she takes them from every room in the house. Then later, the newspaper recycling truck arrives.</p>
<p>Janey opens Harry's door and looks into his room. She always takes his old newspapers or bottles for recycling.</p>
<p>"Ah!" she thinks. "There's a newspaper under Harry's bed."</p>
<p>Janey puts the old newspapers into a black recycling box. She runs from the house and sees the truck.</p>
<p>"Wait!" she says. And she quickly gives the box to one of the men.</p>
<p>Harry is coming back to the house. He sees the recycling truck, and he sees Janey.</p>
<p>"It's Friday!" he says. "The newspaper—! Oh, no!"</p>
<p>Harry chases after the truck.</p>
<p>"Wait! Wait!" he says.</p>
<p>Janey watches him. "What is he doing?" she thinks.</p>
<p>Harry jumps into the back of the truck.</p>
<p>"I want my newspaper!" he says. "Where's my newspaper?"</p>
<p>But there are thousands of newspapers in the truck.</p>
<p>Janey walks quickly from the house to the truck.</p>
<p>"Why is your newspaper important, Harry?" she asks. "It's two days old."</p>
<p>But Harry doesn't hear her. He is thinking, "My million-dollar painting! Where is it?"</p>

<summary>Chapter Three</summary>
<div class="book-page">
  <div class="chapter-label">Chapter Three</div>
  <div class="chapter-title">The Blue Glass</div>
  <div class="chapter-divider"><span> ~ ~ ~ </span></div>
  <div class="book-body">
<p class="first-para">The men from the recycling truck are watching Harry, too. But now Janey is looking at Harry's shoe.</p>
<p>"There's some blue glass in Harry's shoe," Janey thinks. "Where—? Oh!"</p>
<p>Suddenly, she remembers the photo of the blue glass vase on the TV. She looks again at the piece of glass in Harry's shoe.</p>
<p>"Is it from the vase in the art gallery?" she thinks. "Is Harry Black the thief?"</p>
<p>The men from the recycling truck are angry.</p>
<p>"We're going now," they are saying. "We're late."</p>
<p>"But I want my newspaper!" Harry says.</p>
<p>In the house, Janey is talking to the police on the telephone. "Maybe I'm wrong," she is saying. "But there's blue glass in his shoe ... What? ... Yes, he's looking for the newspaper now."</p>
<p>Two policemen arrive quickly.</p>
<p>"Let's look at your shoe," they say to Harry.</p>
<p>Harry doesn't understand. "What's wrong?" he asks.</p>
<p>One of the policemen takes the glass from Harry's shoe.</p>
<p>"This is a very expensive piece of glass," he says. "From a very expensive vase. Remember?"</p>
<p>Suddenly, Harry understands. "Oh, no!" he says.</p>
<p>Police cars and policemen arrive. The men look in the truck for the million-dollar painting. Later, they find the right newspaper—and they find the painting.</p>
<p>"Good work, Janey," one of the policemen says. "And there's a reward."</p>
<p>"Maybe I can buy a painting with the reward!" Janey says. "I like pictures!"</p>

<summary>Проверить понимание текста</summary>

* Кто такой Гарри Блэк и что он делает в художественной галерее в час ночи?
  <details> 
  <summary>ответ:</summary>
  Гарри Блэк — вор (thief). Он залезает в галерею, чтобы украсть картину за миллион долларов.
  </details>

* Что происходит, когда Гарри идёт обратно к окну?
  <details> 
  <summary>ответ:</summary>
  Он натыкается на стол (walks into a table). На столе стоит красивая синяя стеклянная ваза. Она падает на пол и разбивается на сотню кусочков.
  </details>

* Где живёт Гарри и кто его хозяйка?
  <details> 
  <summary>ответ:</summary>
  Он живёт в комнате в пансионе миссис Аллен (Mrs. Allen's rooming house). У неё есть дочь Джейни (Janey).
  </details>

* Куда Гарри прячет украденную картину?
  <details> 
  <summary>ответ:</summary>
  Он заворачивает её в газету и кладёт под кровать (puts it in a newspaper, then puts the newspaper under his bed).
  </details>

* Что Джейни делает по утрам в пятницу?
  <details> 
  <summary>ответ:</summary>
  Она собирает старые газеты и бутылки из каждой комнаты для переработки (recycling).
  </details>

* Какую газету Джейни забирает из комнаты Гарри?
  <details> 
  <summary>ответ:</summary>
  Она забирает газету, которая лежит под кроватью Гарри — ту самую, в которую завернута картина.
  </details>

* Почему Гарри бежит за мусоровозом?
  <details> 
  <summary>ответ:</summary>
  Он хочет вернуть свою газету, потому что в ней находится украденная картина за миллион долларов.
  </details>

* Что Джейни замечает на ботинке Гарри?
  <details> 
  <summary>ответ:</summary>
  Она замечает кусочек синего стекла (blue glass) и вспоминает фото разбитой вазы из галереи по телевизору.
  </details>

* Кого Джейни вызывает и что полицейские находят в ботинке Гарри?
  <details> 
  <summary>ответ:</summary>
  Она вызывает полицию. Полицейские достают из ботинка Гарри очень дорогой кусочек стекла (a very expensive piece of glass) из той самой вазы.
  </details>

* Чем заканчивается история?
  <details> 
  <summary>ответ:</summary>
  Полиция находит картину в мусоровозе. Джейни получает награду (reward) и говорит, что купит на неё картину.
  </details>
</details>
